        YouTube App Link Maker

        A better link for your bio. Give viewers a way to leave the in-app browser and open YouTube.

        Free. No sign-up. Supports watch links, youtu.be, Shorts, playlists, @handles and channel IDs.

        Preview link

        Destination:

        Scan with your phone
        What this can and cannot do

        The share link opens a small page that tries YouTube on phones, with an "Open YouTube app" button for browsers that need a tap. Android uses an app intent; iPhone uses a YouTube URL scheme. Desktop viewers go to normal YouTube.

        Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Threads may block app opens or external-browser launches. No website can force its way out. Viewers may need the browser's menu, then "Open in browser" or Safari / Chrome. The page has a normal YouTube fallback if the app is missing.

        This is a best-effort convenience link, not a guaranteed app opener, subscriber boost or revenue increase. Channel / playlist routing can vary with YouTube app versions. Test your link on your own phone before putting it in your bio.
